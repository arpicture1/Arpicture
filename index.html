Create an ultra-realistic cinematic portrait of a young Indian man in a left-facing side-profile pose, matching the reference photo as closely as possible. Preserve the same facial structure, nose shape, lips, jawline, beard, hairstyle, skin tone and natural proportions. He has messy, slightly wavy black hair with detailed individual strands, a short natural beard and mustache, eyes gently closed, calm and confident expression. He is wearing a plain black T-shirt with a black neckband/earphone around his neck. Use a warm golden-hour sunset background with a smooth orange-to-golden gradient, soft glowing sunlight behind the subject and subtle cinematic rim lighting around the hair and face. Keep the background clean, minimal and softly blurred. Camera framing: close-up portrait from the upper chest to head, side profile, subject facing left, realistic DSLR photography, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, highly detailed hair strands, realistic shadows, cinematic color grading, sharp facial details, professional photography, 4K ultra-realistic quality. Maintain the exact composition and visual feel of the reference image. No extra objects, no text, no watermark, no distortion, no cartoon or anime effect.
